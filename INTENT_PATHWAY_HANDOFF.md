# Intent pathway handoff

`IntentPathway` is a self-contained client component for the homepage. Import `IntentPathway` from `@/components/intent-pathway` and render it where the homepage should present a visitor's next step (the component owns `id="pathways"`).

It includes four keyboard accessible tabs with roving focus, a stable panel area, and route links for business setup, GST/FSSAI, web/app development, and digital marketing. Styling is isolated in `intent-pathway.module.css` and follows the existing global tokens, including both reduced-motion settings.
